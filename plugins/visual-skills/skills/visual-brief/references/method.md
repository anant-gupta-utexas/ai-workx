# Briefing and edits

Adapted from blank-page; licence and revision are in [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).
This is a briefing method, not its motion tool, reference scraper or templates.

For a new brief, choose one coherent look and a sequence of numbered beats.
Make each beat name a subject, action or relationship and what the audience
learns. Include a turn: a reveal, exception, comparison or change in perspective
that advances the explanation. Do not invent a dramatic reversal unsupported
by the subject. Describe the mechanism rather than using an aesthetic adjective
as a substitute for content.

Keep the initial outline near 150 words, before references, evidence and asset
lists. Expand when the task needs more detail; this is an outline discipline,
not a deck engine's required word count. Keep a small edit note near 50 words:
name its target, concrete change and what must remain unchanged. Do not force
a small edit through the entire new-brief process.

`brief.md` should let another author proceed without the conversation:

- Audience, desired decision or understanding, main message and requested format.
- Chosen look and governing style/template/reference, with exact supplied paths.
- Numbered beats, their visual purpose and the turn.
- Visual list mapping each diagram/image to its beat and source asset.
- Factual claims and their actual sources; separate supplied facts, assumptions,
  synthetic examples and unresolved questions. Do not invent quotes or metrics.
- Delivery needs, including native editability, template requirements and any
  backend or evidence still missing.

Use references that actually inform the brief and say what each contributes.
User attachments and existing project evidence can be enough. No fixed quota
of images/motion references, external image scraping or forced reference hunt
is required. Assets are used as supplied; references guide a named aspect of
the result. Do not silently replace a supplied logo, diagram or company template.

When making slides, translate the beats into `slides.md` with one main purpose
per slide and readable titles. Backend-specific syntax belongs to that route's
reference. Keep the factual source list in `brief.md` and relevant notes so it
survives format changes. A brief alone is complete when the user asked for a
brief; when they asked for an export, sources alone are an incomplete handoff.
